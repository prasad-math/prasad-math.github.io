Hi :)

I am a PDE analyst mainly interested in regularity theory, which studies what can be said about an equation without solving it. This is important because most differential equations that arise in applications cannot be solved explicitly. Even when an explicit solution is available, properties of interest in applications, such as positivity, decay, and zeros, may not be readily apparent from its representation.
