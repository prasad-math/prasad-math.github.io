Hi :)

I am a postdoc at Universität Bielefeld, working with Moritz Kassmann. I received my Ph.D. from the TIFR Centre for Applicable Mathematics in 2024, under the supervision of Karthik Adimurthi.

I am a PDE analyst mainly interested in regularity theory, which studies what can be said about an equation without solving it. This is important because most differential equations that arise in applications cannot be solved explicitly and even when an explicit solution is available, properties of interest in applications, such as positivity, decay, and zeros, may not be readily apparent from its representation.

I spend my free time cooking vegan food, reading poetry, making music with my lovely guitar, hiking around the city and the surrounding forests, swimming, and dancing salsa caleña.
